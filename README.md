# Privacy Policy for Slime Crew

**Effective Date:** October 19, 2026

nnwh ("we," "us," or "our") built the Slime Crew app (shown on Google Play as
"Slime Crew: Roguelike Survivor") as a free app. This SERVICE is provided by nnwh at
no cost and is intended for use as is.

Slime Crew is a slime survivor roguelike. The game itself runs on your device
and you can play it offline. **We do not collect any information that
identifies you**: no name, email, account, contacts, location or photos. To
understand how the game is played and to fix crashes, the app uses two Google
services, **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics**, which send
anonymous usage and crash data when your device is online. The app shows no
advertisements and does not use your device's advertising ID.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection,
use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our
Service. If you choose to use our Service, then you agree to the practices
described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

We do **not** collect any personally identifiable information through Slime Crew.
The app does not require an account and does not ask you to sign in.

**Stored on your device only.** Slime Crew keeps a small amount of information
locally, so the game can remember your preferences and progress:

- Your settings (sound and music volume, vibration, and camera shake);
- Your best survival time;
- Which islands you have unlocked and which one you last picked;
- Each slime's mastery progress.

This information never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is
permanently removed when you uninstall the app.

**Sent to Firebase (Google).** When your device is online, the app sends:

- **Game usage (Firebase Analytics):** that the app was opened and for how
  long, and game events such as starting a run, reaching a level, how a run
  ended (the island, level reached, number of enemies defeated and time
  survived), and unlocking an island; plus general device information such as
  the device model, operating system version, app version, language and
  country (estimated from the IP address, which Google does not store).
- **Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics):** when the app crashes or hits an
  error, a report of what went wrong (the technical stack trace, the island
  and level being played at the time) with device information such as the
  model, operating system version, free memory and disk space, and whether the
  app was in the foreground.

Both services identify an installation of the app with a random identifier
(the Firebase app instance ID and the Crashlytics installation ID). These
identifiers are not linked to your name or any account, and a new one is
created if you reinstall the app. We **do not** collect the Android
advertising ID, and the app does not request the advertising ID permission.

We use this information only to understand which parts of the game are
played, to balance the game, and to find and fix crashes. We do not sell it,
we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with anyone except
Google, which processes it for us as described below.

**Device permissions.** On Android the app uses:

- **Vibration**, to buzz your phone when your slime is hurt (you can turn it
  off in the game's settings);
- **Internet and network state**, only so Firebase can send the usage and
  crash data described above.

The app does not request location, contacts, the camera, the microphone, or
your files.

## 2. Third-Party Services

- **Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics** (Google LLC) — usage
  statistics and crash reporting, as described in section 1. Google processes
  this data on our behalf. Firebase's privacy information:
  https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and Google's privacy policy:
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Game engine** — the game is built with Godot Engine, which runs inside the
  app and sends nothing anywhere.
- **Fonts, artwork, music and sounds** — bundled inside the app and loaded
  locally, with no network request.
- **Google Play** — the app is distributed through Google Play, which
  operates under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 3. Advertising

The current version of Slime Crew **does not show any advertisements** and
does not collect the advertising ID.

A future version may offer optional rewarded ads (an ad you choose to watch in
return for an in-game reward). If that happens, this policy will be updated
**before** that version is released, to describe exactly which provider is used,
what it collects, and how you can control it. Ads would never be shown without
you choosing to watch one.

## 4. Log Data

When Slime Crew crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics collects the
crash data described in section 1 (Log Data) so we can fix the problem. It does
not include your name, contacts, or anything you type.

## 5. Service Providers

We use Google (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics) as a service
provider to collect and analyse the usage and crash data described above on
our behalf. Google is obligated to use this data only to provide those services
to us. Analytics event data is kept for no longer than 14 months and
Crashlytics crash reports for 90 days, after which they are deleted.

## 6. Security

We value your trust. Your game progress stays on your device. The usage and
crash data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We nevertheless
encourage you to protect your device using the security features it provides.

## 7. Links to Other Sites

Slime Crew contains no links that open websites. Its credits screen names the
websites of the people and projects that made the game's art and engine, as
plain text only.

## 8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children
under 13. The usage and crash data the app sends does not identify the player.

Slime Crew is a general-audience game with cartoon fantasy violence and is not
directed to children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has
provided information to us, please contact us so that we can address it.

## 9. Your Choices and Deleting Your Data

You can stop the app from sending usage and crash data by playing with your
device offline, and you can reset the identifiers by uninstalling and
reinstalling the app. Because the data is not linked to your identity, we
cannot look it up by name or email; if you would like us to delete the data
from your installation, contact us and we will explain how to find your app
instance ID so we can delete it.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to
review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes
by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page, and by updating the Effective
Date at the top. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted
on this page.
